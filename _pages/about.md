## About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk/), part of the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), where I am advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with particular interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**: Synthesizing verifiable reasoning data at scale.
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: Understanding the perception bottleneck for chart understanding and developing inner representation-based hallucination mitigation.
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**: Investigating universal truthfulness hyperplanes inside LLMs.
